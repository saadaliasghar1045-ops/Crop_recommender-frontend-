ABOUT CROP RECOMMENDER

Technology that helps farmers decide.

Crop Recommender is a machine learning project designed to explore how data-driven technology can assist with crop selection based on soil and environmental conditions.

THE IDEA

Built around a real problem.

Choosing what to grow can depend on many factors. Soil nutrients, temperature, humidity, pH, and rainfall can all influence which crops are suitable for a field.

Crop Recommender was created as a practical machine learning project to turn these conditions into a simple, understandable recommendation.
